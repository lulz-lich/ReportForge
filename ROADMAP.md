# Roadmap

ReportForge is already usable as a portfolio-grade MVP. Future safe improvements could include:

- More language templates.
- Optional custom branding through local configuration.
- DOCX export.
- Richer PDF styling and cover pages.
- Evidence attachment manifest generation.
- Template snapshot tests.
- Optional SBOM and release automation.

Out of scope remains unchanged: no scanning, exploitation, credential collection, persistence, evasion, or destructive behavior.
